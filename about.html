    About TechMobiles

    TechMobiles & Accessories is an innovative business that sells premium mobile devices and accessories at affordable prices. We are committed to providing high-quality products and exceptional customer service.

    Our Vision

    To become South Africa's leading mobile accessory brand with a strong digital footprint and nationwide presence by 2027.

    Our Mission

    To provide high-quality, affordable mobile accessories that enhance our customers' digital lifestyle while delivering exceptional service and building lasting relationships.

    Our Target Audience

    Tech-savvy individuals aged 18–45, including students, professionals, and casual shoppers who value quality and innovation in mobile accessories.

    Goals and SMART Objectives

    Goals: Establish a professional and modern online presence. Enable online browsing of products and promotions. Generate leads and enquiries via forms. Drive foot traffic to the physical store.

    SMART Objectives

    SMART Objectives:

    • Achieve 1,000 unique website visits per month within the first three months
    • Receive at least 50 product enquiries per month via the enquiry form
    • Maintain a bounce rate under 40% for product pages

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is your return policy?

    We offer a 30-day return policy for all unused products in original packaging. Returns are free for defective items.

    Do you offer international shipping?

    Currently, we only ship within South Africa. We're working on expanding our shipping options in the future.

    How long does delivery take?

    Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days within major cities, and 5-7 days for other areas. Express delivery is available for major centers.

    Are your products covered by warranty?

    Yes, all our products come with a 1-year manufacturer's warranty. Accessories have a 6-month warranty period.

    Do you offer bulk discounts?

    Yes, we offer competitive wholesale pricing for bulk orders. Please use our enquiry form for wholesale inquiries.
